# Shelly: design file (v1)

An original REELS/FAB cartoon character. `shelly.svg` is the master vector, and `shelly.jpg` (787×1400) is the reference used in production. Do not change her proportions or colors without updating this file.

## Identity
- A friendly cartoon turtle, warm and playful, for family content and products tied to the store.
- Catchphrase: "Slow and steady, let's make something!"
- Voice: Kokoro `af_sky` at 108% speed (playful and warm).

## Shape
- Large round head (about 1.1× the width of the shell's chest) with two big oval eyes. Each pupil has two white highlights. Short curved brows.
- Soft pink cheeks and an open smile with a pink tongue.
- A yellow five-petal flower with a coral center on the right side of her head.
- A coral scarf with a single knotted tail on her left.
- A teal oval shell behind her body with lighter hexagonal scutes on the sides.
- A yellow belly plate with horizontal segments and one vertical line.
- Short arms (the right one waves) and two oval feet with three dots on each.

## Rules
- Flat 2D cartoon style with thick outlines. No realistic rendering and no human features.
- Always appears with the flower and the scarf.
- Content is for families and children, with no misleading claims. Disclose "AI-generated" and #ad when promoting products.
